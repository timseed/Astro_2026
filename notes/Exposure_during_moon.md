# Full Moon Rules

  - Try and image as far away from the moon as possible
  - Targets
    - DO 
      - Open Cluster 
      - Narrow Band Ha 
      - Exposure 30s > 1m is fine 

    - DONT 
      - Nebulae 
      - Long Exposure > 1m
